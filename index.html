01 / THE INSTITUTE

Where AI governance meets security engineering.

AI risk can no longer be treated as a policy problem alone. Effective governance requires standards, technical controls, threat-informed design, rigorous assurance and continuous testing.

IAIGSRM brings these disciplines together in an independent institute model focused on research, education and practical engagement with public, private and non-profit organizations.

02 / FIVE FOCUS AREAS

A unified approach to AI risk.

01

AI Governance

Operating models, accountability, lifecycle governance, human oversight, policy and enterprise AI risk management.

02

AI Standards & Assurance

Alignment and assessment across ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, emerging regulation and control frameworks.

03

AI Cybersecurity

Security architecture and controls for models, data, RAG, agents, infrastructure, identities and AI supply chains.

04

AI Threat Modeling

Threat-informed analysis connecting AI attack vectors, system impacts and layered mitigating controls.

05

AI Red Teaming

Adversarial testing for prompt injection, data leakage, unsafe agency, model abuse and control effectiveness.

03 / ENGAGEMENT MODEL

From strategy to sustained operation.

Advise

Assess risk, define governance, interpret standards and establish priorities.

Design

Develop architectures, control frameworks, threat models, operating models and assurance programs.

Deliver

Implement controls, assessments, testing programs, governance processes and technical safeguards.

Operate

Support continuous assurance, monitoring, control validation, red teaming and governance maturation.
