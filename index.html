01 / ABOUT

Where AI governance meets security engineering.

AI risk can no longer be treated as a policy problem alone. Effective governance requires standards, technical controls, threat-informed design, rigorous assurance and continuous testing.

IAIGSRM brings these disciplines together in an independent institute model focused on research, education and practical engagement with public, private and non-profit organizations.

04 / RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

Research intended to become practice.

IAIGSRM develops applied research at the intersection of AI governance, cybersecurity, risk, ethics and assurance, emphasizing methods organizations can implement and evaluate.

Explore the IAIGSRM AI Governance, Security & Risk Management Framework, integrating AI Readiness, Audit & Assurance, and Control Delivery. Its three-tier architecture connects threat scenarios, baseline controls mapped to NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, ISO/IEC 27002:2022 and ISO/IEC 42001:2023, and enhanced safeguards. A separate AI red teaming capability is informed by Japan AISI methodology.

06 / FOUNDER

Stephen Buerle, PhD, CISSP

Founder, Executive Director & Head of Research

Stephen Buerle brings experience in cybersecurity, enterprise security architecture, AI governance, assurance, and applied AI research. His work spans penetration testing, security engineering, controls development, risk management, and expert systems and machine learning. He has worked with the IAEA, Unisys, JPMorgan Chase, IBM, and Kyndryl, and holds graduate degrees from Harvard, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, and the University at Albany.
